You always know what’s coming next.

Every plan needs a rhythm, not just a strategy. This is the actual cadence of work behind your relationship with Lume Capital — season by season, discipline by discipline — so nothing is left for a once-a-year surprise.

Two scheduled meetings. Continuous advice in between.

Our work together follows a deliberate rhythm — not a reactive one. One meeting in spring, one in fall, each with its own focus.

SPRING · JAN–APR

Spring Planning

Strategy & portfolio. The meeting where we step back and look at the whole picture.

  • Life updates
  • Plan update
  • Retirement planning
  • Tax planning
  • Liability management
  • Education funding
  • Portfolio review
FALL · SEP–DEC

Fall Review

Tax & benefits. The meeting where we close out the year with intention.

  • Charitable giving
  • Tax withholding
  • IRA / Roth strategy
  • Savings strategy
  • Deferred compensation
  • Benefits & health care

Between meetings, reach us any time — questions large or small, planned or unexpected.

See what we coordinate across both meetings

The year behind the two meetings.

Early Year sets up Spring Planning. Year-End sets up Fall Review. Mid-Year bridges the two, as your life requires.

EY

Early Year · Jan–Apr

Asset allocation, retirement income, tax-return season, and Roth conversion decisions — the work that frames Spring Planning.

MY

Mid-Year · May–Aug

Insurance, Medicare enrollment, and estate-document reviews — the bridge season, run as your life requires rather than the calendar.

YE

Year-End · Sep–Dec

Charitable giving, RMDs, gifting, and next year’s income projections — the work that frames Fall Review.

Nothing here waits for the next scheduled meeting to get noticed — it’s simply when we make sure it happens.

See where this fits in our five-stage process

A complete financial picture.

Every planning activity on this page falls under one of these disciplines — and they are worked together, on one plan, rather than managed in isolation from each other.

Investments

Investments & retirement income — reviewed every year against your actual plan, not a benchmark.

Asset Protection

Insurance & risk management — health, home, life, disability and long-term care, kept current as your life changes.

Tax Planning

Tax planning & education — the most active discipline on the calendar, with touchpoints in nearly every season.

Estate Planning

Wills, trusts & titling — reviewed on a set schedule so nothing goes stale for years at a time.

Charitable

Giving strategy & donor-advised funds — structured so generosity is as deliberate as the rest of the plan.

General

Goal tracking & wealth forecasting — revisited at every meeting, not just once a year.

VFO / Professional Network

Coordinating your outside advisors — your CPA, attorney and specialists, kept working from the same plan.
